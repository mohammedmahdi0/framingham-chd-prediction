CONCLUSIONS
- Without pruning the tree has 497 leaves: train accuracy 1.0000 but CV accuracy only 0.7501
  -> clear over-fitting, exactly as described in the lecture.
- Pruning removes the over-fitting: the best variant (2) Pre-pruning (depth, leaf size)) has 4 leaves,
  train 0.8497 and CV 0.8481 (+0.0979 over the unpruned tree).
- Even the best pruned tree stays close to the baseline (0.8481) and below logistic regression,
  so a single tree is not the final model. Pruning fixes over-fitting; it cannot add information.
