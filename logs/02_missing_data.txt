CONCLUSIONS
- Best strategy for logistic regression: Median (0.8559).
- Dropping incomplete rows throws away 14% of the training data and is among the weakest options.
- The differences are tiny (smaller than the CV standard deviation, about 0.005).
DECISION: median imputation (simple, and the best or tied-best).
